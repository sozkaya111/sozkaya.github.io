01 / About

Human questions.
Computational tools.

I’m an undergraduate at Carnegie Mellon University studying Cognitive Science and Computer Science, with a concentration in Human-Computer Interaction and a minor in Creative Writing. My interests sit at the intersection of human-computer interaction, artificial intelligence, and cognitive science.

Through work at Carnegie Mellon, Columbia, Stanford, Koç University, and Pitt/UPMC, I’ve explored machine learning, social reasoning, visual perception, and human-robot interaction. Across those projects, I’ve become especially interested in how technology represents and responds to human behavior — and how those systems can be made more understandable, useful, and human-centered.
